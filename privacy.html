WikiMatch Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WikiMatch handles information when you use the app. WikiMatch is designed as a Wikipedia-based game and does not use your data for tracking.

Back to Support
No tracking WikiMatch does not track you across apps or websites.
Limited analytics Only limited, non-linked analytics data is collected.
No user account required WikiMatch does not require account registration to play.

Information Collected

Device Identifier
WikiMatch may collect a device identifier for analytics purposes. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.
Product Interaction
WikiMatch may collect information about how you interact with the app, such as app usage events, for analytics purposes. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

How Information Is Used

Analytics only
The limited data described above is used only to understand app performance and improve the game experience.
Not linked to you
Based on the app's privacy configuration, the collected analytics data is not linked to your identity.
Not used for tracking
WikiMatch does not use collected information to track you across apps, websites, or services owned by other companies.

Local Storage and App Functionality

UserDefaults
WikiMatch uses Apple's UserDefaults API for basic app functionality, such as saving local preferences or small pieces of app state on your device.
Wikipedia content
WikiMatch depends on Wikipedia article content and links to provide gameplay. When you use the app, requests may be made to retrieve Wikipedia content needed for the game.

Children's Privacy

No intentional collection of directly identifying information
WikiMatch does not ask players to create accounts or intentionally collect directly identifying personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or home address through the app itself.

Changes to This Policy

Updates
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
