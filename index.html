Support for WikiMatch

WikiMatch is a fast Wikipedia navigation game. One player picks a target article, everyone starts from the same random page, and the first player to reach the target by tapping links wins.

Contact Support Read Privacy Policy
Quick rounds Start from the same random page and race toward the target.

Built around Wikipedia Article availability and content depend on Wikipedia and network access.

How to Play

1
Pick a target article
The host chooses the Wikipedia article everyone is trying to reach.
2
Start from the same random page
All players begin on the same randomly selected Wikipedia article.
3
Navigate by tapping links
Move from article to article using only links available on each page.
4
Reach the target first
The first player to land on the target article wins the round.

Common Questions

Why is an article unavailable or different from another device?
WikiMatch relies on Wikipedia content. Articles, links, redirects, and page availability may change over time or differ by language and region.
Does the game require an internet connection?
Yes. WikiMatch depends on live Wikipedia article content and navigation links.
What should I send when reporting a problem?
Please include the app version, your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
